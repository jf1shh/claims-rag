## Short version

1. **The probe's first verdict was wrong.** "5 of 5 variants diverged" came mostly from how the
   probe was set up, not from the names. Two confounds were found and fixed in
   `eval/build_fairness_probe.py` (identity-control baseline, surname in filenames).
2. **With the confounds removed, one decision still moves with the name.** The SIU "Chronology"
   red flag (claim filed within 10 days of policy inception) was raised in 9 of 9 runs with the
   unchanged name and in 0 to 7 of 10 runs for each renamed variant. Two names were never flagged.
3. **The flag is not supported by the documents.** The dossier has no policy inception date. Two
   control runs invented one ("assumed to be January 8, 2026, based on the case study"). The
   variants that say "not flagged" or "undetermined" are the more correct answers.
4. **What this does and doesn't show.** The free-text SIU answer on this claim is unstable and
   sensitive to the claimant's name. It does **not** show bias against any group. That would take
   more claims, more names, more runs and real data. Five names and one claim can't tell a
   demographic pattern from a fragile answer that any name change knocks loose.

## Confound 1: the baseline went through a different ingest path

v1 compared each variant against the original claim. `ingest_all.py` ingests the original from parsed
PDF/XLSX files. The probe ingested variants as raw text under new claim IDs and `_fairness-probe_`
filenames. The original answered in one layout ("#### 1. **Chronology:**") in all 6 runs. Every probe
copy, including the one with the **name unchanged**, answered in another ("### **SIU Review Flag
Analysis for Claim ID …**"). So the exact-text diff flagged every variant, whatever its name. Fix: the
baseline is now an identity control, the base name sent through the variant path.

## Confound 2: variant filenames kept the base surname

In v2, variant documents were named like `police_theft_report_Chen_fairness-probe_wei_zhang.pdf`
while their text said "Wei Zhang". Only the control's cited filenames agreed with its text. Fix:
`build_variant_documents` swaps the surname in the filename too. v3 is the first clean run.

## What to do with this

- **Grounding fix, not a name filter.** The answer should say "undetermined: no policy inception date
  in the file" whatever the name. `backend/claims_tools.py` already has a deterministic SIU checklist
  that takes structured inputs. Routing chronology through it, or requiring the date before the flag
  can be raised, removes this failure for every claimant. Follow-up, not in this change.
- **Rerun the probe after that fix** and expect every row to read N/U. Then widen it: more claims, a
  larger roster, more runs.
- Gap 3 in [docs/governance/naic-ai-bulletin-map.md](../../docs/governance/naic-ai-bulletin-map.md)
  stays **Partial**. A probe has now run, and its useful output is a found defect, not a clean bill.
