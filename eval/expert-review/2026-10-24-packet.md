### Third-Party Diminished Value Calculation

**Answer:**  
Third-party diminished value claims are calculated using the following formula:

1. **Base Loss**: 10% of the vehicle's pre-loss Actual Cash Value (ACV).
2. **Damage Modifier**: Applied based on the severity of damage:
   - Severe structural damage: 1.00
   - Major structural and panel damage: 0.75
   - Moderate panel damage: 0.50
   - Minor panel damage: 0.25
   - No structural or panel damage: 0.00
3. **Mileage Modifier**: Applied based on the vehicle's mileage:
   - Under 20,000 miles: 1.00
   - 20,000–39,999 miles: 0.80
   - 40,000–59,999 miles: 0.60
   - 60,000–79,999 miles: 0.40
   - 80,000–99,999 miles: 0.20
   - 100,000 miles or more: 0.00

**Formula:**
> **Diminished Value = (10% of ACV) × Damage Modifier × Mileage Modifier**
