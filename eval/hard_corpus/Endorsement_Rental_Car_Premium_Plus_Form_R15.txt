Endorsement Form R15: Premium Plus Rental Vehicle Rider
1. Rider Benefit
Form R15 raises the daily rental reimbursement limit to $65 per day and allows a full-size SUV or a luxury sedan while the primary vehicle is in the repair shop.
2. Period of Repair rule
Rental reimbursement runs for the actual time of active repairs, up to 45 days. Delays caused by backordered parts extend the limit by up to 10 days when the claims manager has approved the delay in writing.
