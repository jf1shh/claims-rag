STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: VEHICLE STORAGE FEES (2025 EDITION - SUPERSEDED)
Status: Superseded effective 2026-01-01 by the 2026 Vehicle Storage Fees SOP. Kept for audit of claims opened in 2025.
1. Covered Storage
Reasonable storage charges at a tow yard or repair facility are covered as part of a covered collision or comprehensive loss, up to $35 per day.
2. Notice and Cut-Off
The adjuster must notify the policyholder and the facility in writing when the vehicle is ready to be moved. Storage accruing more than 5 calendar days after that notice is the policyholder's responsibility.
3. Moving the Vehicle
To stop accruing charges, the adjuster should arrange transfer of a total-loss vehicle to the salvage vendor's yard within 5 business days of the total loss declaration.
