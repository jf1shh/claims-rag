SOP: Labor Rates & Repair Shop Guidelines (2025 EDITION - SUPERSEDED)
Status: Superseded effective 2026-01-01 by the 2026 Labor Rates SOP. Applies only to estimates written in the 2025 calendar year.
1. Standard Repair Labor Rates
Body shop labor claims were subject to the following capped hourly labor rates for the 2025 calendar year:
- Sheet Metal Repair: $58 per hour
- Frame Alignment: $70 per hour
- Refinishing / Painting: $58 per hour
- Mechanical Work: $98 per hour
- Aluminum Structural Repair: $105 per hour
2. Paint & Material Calculations
Paint materials were capped at $38 per refinish hour.
