SOP: Total Loss Vehicle Valuation (2025 EDITION - SUPERSEDED)
Status: Superseded effective 2026-01-01 by the 2026 Total Loss Valuation SOP.
1. Total Loss Threshold Rule
Most states enforce a statutory total loss threshold. In California, the Total Loss Formula (Cost of Repair + Salvage Value >= ACV) was used. In Texas, a flat threshold of 100% of ACV was applied. In Florida, the threshold was 75% of ACV.
2. Valuation Methodology
Claims handlers determined ACV using one valuation source, with a second source required only above $40,000 ACV.
