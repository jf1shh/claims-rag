SOP: Owner-Retained Salvage
1. Retention Option
When a vehicle is declared a total loss, the policyholder may keep the vehicle. The settlement is reduced by the vehicle's salvage bid value, which the adjuster must obtain from the approved salvage vendor in writing.
2. Required Steps
The adjuster must confirm that the title will be re-branded as Salvage before the policyholder takes possession, notify the policyholder that the vehicle cannot be driven on public roads until it passes a state inspection, and release the payment within 7 calendar days of the signed retention form.
