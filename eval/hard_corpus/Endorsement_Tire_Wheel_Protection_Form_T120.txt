Endorsement Form T120: Tire and Wheel Protection Rider (Legacy)
1. Benefit
Legacy Form T120 pays to repair or replace tires and wheels damaged by road hazards such as potholes and debris, up to $600 per wheel assembly and $1,500 per policy year. The deductible is $100 per claim. Form T120 is no longer sold; existing policies keep it until renewal.
2. Exclusions
Cosmetic curb rash is not covered. Tires with less than 4/32 inch of tread remaining before the loss are not covered.
