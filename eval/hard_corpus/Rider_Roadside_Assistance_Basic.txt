Policy Rider: Roadside Assistance Basic
1. Covered Roadside Emergencies
The Roadside Assistance Basic rider provides business-hours service (7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.) for: towing following mechanical breakdown, battery jumpstarts, flat tire changes using the policyholder's spare, and emergency fuel delivery of up to 1 gallon of gasoline. Lockout assistance is not included.
2. Distance Limits & Towing Caps
Towing under the Basic rider is capped at 10 miles or a maximum of $50 per event.
3. Exclusion of Accidental Towing
Towing after a collision is handled under the Collision coverage of the primary policy.
