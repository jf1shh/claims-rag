Endorsement Form T210: Tire and Wheel Protection Rider
1. Benefit
Form T210 pays to repair or replace tires and wheels damaged by road hazards such as potholes, debris and curbs, up to $1,000 per wheel assembly and $2,500 per policy year. The deductible is $50 per claim.
2. Exclusions
Cosmetic curb rash that does not affect function is not covered. Tires with less than 3/32 inch of tread remaining before the loss are reimbursed at 50% of the repair or replacement cost.
