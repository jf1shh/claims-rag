# Glossary

- **Evidence:** Exact authorized document excerpts supplied to or returned from retrieval, identified by stable document and chunk metadata.
- **Interpretation:** An explanation, calculation, assumption, uncertainty label, or refusal derived from evidence.
- **Decision boundary:** The explicit separation between assistant research and a human or external system recording a claims decision.
- **Claim dossier:** Documents scoped to one claim, such as reports, estimates, receipts, or telematics records.
- **Global policy document:** A guideline, endorsement, statute, schedule, or adjuster reference not attached to one claim.
- **RAG:** Retrieval-augmented generation: retrieve source passages before generating an answer.
- **ICM:** Interpretable Context Methodology, a staged workflow and handoff layer that makes context, checkpoints, audits, and outputs visible.
- **Guardrail:** A mechanically tested rule that prevents a known failure mode.
- **Sensor:** An advisory check that reports a condition without blocking a merge.
